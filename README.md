<sub>PL. 0 — LEGEND</sub>

1. ***a luz*** — flying insects, moths included, don't fly at the light: they turn their backs to it, as if it were the sky, and over a lamp they flip and dive.<sup>1</sup>
2. ***psykhé*** (ψυχή) — Greek for soul and mind; also, butterfly or moth.<sup>2</sup>
3. ***le Fou*** — the figure from my avatar, mid-air. In tarot, the card without a number, often read as the beginning; in chess, the piece that moves on the diagonal.
4. ***o mundo*** — card XXI. In the 15th-century Visconti-Sforza deck, The World is a globe with a city inside.<sup>3</sup>
5. ***apofenia*** — seeing meaningful connections between unrelated things; a term from psychiatry.<sup>4</sup> The method is to see the pattern and then test it. The test is below.
